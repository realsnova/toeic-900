**每一題請看三件事：**
1. 標示的答案是不是唯一正確的？有沒有另一個選項也說得通？

3. 解析對嗎、看得懂嗎？

## S-01（P5-274）

**Customers ______ to return items must present the original receipt.**

- (A) wished
- (B) wishes
- (C) wishing
- (D) wish

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(C) wishing

解析：現在分詞 wishing 修飾 customers（= who wish to）。句子的動詞是 must present，所以空格不能再放動詞 wish。

</details>

## S-02（P5-245）

**The new terminal is expected to be fully ______ by the end of the year.**

- (A) operation
- (B) operational
- (C) operate
- (D) operationally

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(B) operational

解析：be 動詞後接形容詞，fully 修飾它：fully operational（全面啟用的）。operation 是名詞；operate 是動詞；operationally 是副詞。

</details>

## S-03（P5-271）

**______ in 1952, the hotel has hosted many famous guests.**

- (A) Building
- (B) Built
- (C) To build
- (D) Having built

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(B) Built

解析：飯店是「被建造」，用過去分詞 Built 開頭的分詞構句。Building、Having built 是主動，表示飯店自己蓋東西，語意不合。

</details>

## S-04（P5-255）

**______ she lacked formal training, Ms. Diaz quickly became the team's top analyst.**

- (A) Although
- (B) Because
- (C) Despite
- (D) Unless

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(A) Although

解析：Although + 子句（雖然）：雖然沒受過正式訓練，卻很快成為最好的分析師。Despite 是介系詞，不能接子句；Because、Unless 語意不合。

</details>

## S-07（P7-14-3）

題組：單篇：線上討論（新制・多人聊天）

```
Daniel Reyes [2:03 P.M.]
Quick update on the Lumina launch event. The venue just told me the main hall is double-booked on May 18.

Sophie Grant [2:04 P.M.]
Seriously? We've already printed the invitations.

Daniel Reyes [2:05 P.M.]
They've offered us the Garden Pavilion instead, same date, at no extra charge. It holds about 150 people.

Marcus Webb [2:06 P.M.]
We have 210 confirmed guests.

Daniel Reyes [2:07 P.M.]
That's the problem. The main hall is available on May 25, though.

Sophie Grant [2:09 P.M.]
Moving the date means sending a correction to everyone, but at least the invitations don't list the room.

Aiko Tanaka [2:10 P.M.]
I can handle the correction notices. If we decide by tomorrow, they'll go out on Thursday.

Daniel Reyes [2:11 P.M.]
Let's go with May 25. I'll confirm with the venue this afternoon.
```

**What does Ms. Grant indicate about the invitations?**

- (A) They have not been printed yet.
- (B) They were designed by Ms. Tanaka.
- (C) They were mailed on Thursday.
- (D) They do not mention the specific room.

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(D) They do not mention the specific room.

解析：Grant 說 at least the invitations don't list the room（至少邀請函上沒寫是哪個廳）。她在 2:04 也說邀請函已經印好了。

</details>

## S-08（P7-13-2）

題組：單篇：簡訊對話（新制・意圖題）

```
Karen Liu (9:12 A.M.)
Hi Tom, are you still at the Westfield office? The projector in Conference Room B won't connect to my laptop, and the client arrives at 10.

Tom Becker (9:14 A.M.)
I left about twenty minutes ago. Did you try the adapter in the top drawer of the cabinet?

Karen Liu (9:15 A.M.)
That drawer is locked.

Tom Becker (9:16 A.M.)
Ah, right. Ask Priya at the front desk. She keeps the spare keys.

Karen Liu (9:17 A.M.)
Will do. Thanks!
```

**At 9:15 A.M., what does Ms. Liu most likely mean when she writes, "That drawer is locked"?**

- (A) She cannot get the adapter Mr. Becker mentioned.
- (B) She plans to move the meeting to another room.
- (C) She has already found the adapter.
- (D) She thinks Mr. Becker took the adapter with him.

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(A) She cannot get the adapter Mr. Becker mentioned.

解析：意圖題：Becker 建議用抽屜裡的轉接頭，Liu 回答抽屜鎖著，意思是她拿不到那個轉接頭。接著 Becker 才告訴她去找保管備用鑰匙的 Priya。

</details>

## S-09（P7-19-4）

**When will Mr. Hassan be interviewed?**

## S-10（L2-025）

（聽力 Part 2：考試時只聽到聲音，不會看到文字）

**Where did you put the quarterly sales report?**

- (A) Sales were about three percent higher.
- (B) I emailed it to you this morning.
- (C) It was quite detailed and well written.

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(B) I emailed it to you this morning.

解析：Where 問報告放哪裡，間接回答「今天早上寄給你了」。另外兩個描述報告內容，沒回答在哪裡。

## S-11（L2-001）

（聽力 Part 2：考試時只聽到聲音，不會看到文字）

**Where should I submit my expense report?**

- (A) Through the expense portal on the company intranet.
- (B) The expenses were higher than last month's.
- (C) About fifty dollars for the taxi and meals.

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(A) Through the expense portal on the company intranet.

解析：問「在哪裡交」，答「透過公司內部網站的報帳系統」。「About fifty dollars…」答的是金額；「The expenses were higher…」用 expense 做同字陷阱，沒回答在哪裡交。

</details>

## S-12（L2-058）

（聽力 Part 2：考試時只聽到聲音，不會看到文字）

**Where did the shipment get held up?**

- (A) For over a week, unfortunately.
- (B) Somewhere at the border crossing, I heard.
- (C) It was packed in sealed containers.

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(B) Somewhere at the border crossing, I heard.

解析：Where 問貨在哪裡被卡住，回答「聽說是在邊境關口」。「For over a week」答時間長度；「It was packed…」描述包裝。

</details>
