## T-01（L2-81）

（聽力 Part 2：考試時只聽到聲音）

**When will the shipment from Korea arrive?**

- (A) It's been delayed at customs.
- (B) By air freight.
- (C) Korea has great food.

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(A) It's been delayed at customs.

解析：When 問何時到，間接回答「在海關被耽擱了」。「By air freight」答運送方式；「Korea has great food」用 Korea 做聯想。

## T-02（L2-99）

（聽力 Part 2：考試時只聽到聲音）

**Why don't you ask Mr. Kim for advice?**

- (A) I asked for the bill and paid it at the front counter.
- (B) Because he gave me some advice.
- (C) He's out of the office this week.

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(C) He's out of the office this week.

解析：Why don't you…? 是建議，間接回答「他這週不在辦公室」（沒辦法問）。用 Because 回答建議是常見陷阱；最後一個用 ask 做同字陷阱，答非所問。

</details>

## T-04（L34-14-3）

題組：對話：訪客問路（圖表題）

```
女：Hi, I'm here for the two o'clock interview with Ms. Grant in human resources. Could you tell me where her office is?

```

圖表：

```
BUILDING DIRECTORY
Floor 2 ... Marketing
Floor 3 ... Customer Service
Floor 4 ... Accounting / Legal
Floor 5 ... Executive Offices
```

**What does the man ask the woman to do first?**

- (A) Call Ms. Grant
- (B) Take the stairs
- (C) Sign in at the desk

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(C) Sign in at the desk

解析：男士說 first, you'll need to sign in here（要先在這裡登記），並配戴訪客證。

## T-06（L34-20-2）

題組：對話：簡報提前（意圖題）

```
男：Hi, Maria. The client from Lindell Foods just called. They want to move tomorrow's presentation from three P.M. to ten A.M.
女：Ten in the morning? The slides still need the updated sales figures.
男：I know. Finance said they'd send us the numbers by the end of today.
女：Well, I've got the evening free.
男：Thanks. I'll let the client know we can do ten, then.
```

**What does the woman imply when she says, "Well, I've got the evening free"?**

- (A) She would like to leave work early.
- (B) She can update the slides tonight.
- (C) She cannot attend the presentation.
- (D) She wants to meet the client in the evening.

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(B) She can update the slides tonight.

解析：意圖題：財務部今天下班前才會給數字，女士說她今晚有空，意思是她可以今晚把數字更新到簡報上，所以十點沒問題。

</details>

## T-08（P6-1-4）

文章：電子郵件：訂單延遲通知

```
To: Rebecca Liu <r.liu@stellaroffice.com>
From: Customer Care <care@apexsupplies.com>
Subject: Order #58214

Dear Ms. Liu,

Thank you for your recent order of ergonomic office chairs. Unfortunately, due to an unexpected surge in demand, the model you selected is temporarily out of stock. We expect to receive a new shipment ___[1]___ ten business days.

___[2]___, we would like to offer you two options. You may either wait for the original model or switch to the Deluxe series at no additional cost. The Deluxe series has received excellent reviews and offers ___[3]___ lumbar support.

___[4]___ We apologize for any inconvenience and appreciate your patience.

Sincerely,
Apex Supplies Customer Care
```

**空格 [4] 應填入：**

- (A) Please let us know your preference by Friday.
- (B) Your original order has already been shipped.
- (C) The Deluxe series costs $40 more per chair.
- (D) Unfortunately, we are unable to offer you any alternatives.

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(A) Please let us know your preference by Friday.

解析：句子插入題：前面提供兩個選擇（等原型號，或免費改成 Deluxe 系列），接著請對方週五前告知選擇最連貫。訂單已出貨和「延遲」矛盾；Deluxe 要加價和「不另收費」矛盾；無法提供替代方案和前面給的兩個選擇矛盾。

</details>

## T-09（P6-8-3）

文章：報導：本地餐廳擴張

```
RIVERTON — Bella Cucina, a family-owned Italian restaurant, announced this week that it will open a second location downtown. The new branch, ___[1]___ is scheduled to open in October, will seat up to eighty guests.

Owner Marco Bellini said the decision came after years of ___[2]___ demand. "Our regular customers have been asking us to expand for a long time," he said. ___[3]___ The new location will feature an open kitchen and a private dining room for events.

The restaurant is currently ___[4]___ experienced chefs and servers. Interested applicants can apply through the company website.
```

**空格 [3] 應填入：**

- (A) He added that the menu will remain largely the same.
- (B) The new location will open in March.
- (C) Mr. Bellini said customers rarely visit the restaurant.
- (D) The restaurant has decided not to expand after all.

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(A) He added that the menu will remain largely the same.

解析：句子插入題：前一句是老闆說常客一直希望擴點，接著補充「菜單大致不變」最連貫。三月開幕和十月開幕矛盾；客人很少和「需求多年」矛盾；不擴點和整篇報導矛盾。

</details>

## T-10（P5-107）

**Attendance at the trade fair rose ______ after the organizers lowered ticket prices.**

- (A) sharp
- (B) sharply
- (C) sharpness
- (D) sharpen

<details><summary>看答案與解析</summary>

答案：(B) sharply

解析：修飾動詞 rose，要用副詞 sharply（急遽地）。sharp 是形容詞；sharpen 是動詞；sharpness 是名詞。

</details>
