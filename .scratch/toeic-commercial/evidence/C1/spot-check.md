# C1 練習題抽檢（17 題＋20 個單字）

請看每題的題目、正解和詳解，覺得有問題的寫下編號和原因；都沒問題就回覆「都OK」。
規則：抽到 1 題答案錯、或 2 題以上有問題，整批退回重做。

### 1. Part 5（介係詞）

**Suppliers may receive early payment ______ a two percent discount on the invoice total.**

- ✅ in exchange for
- in return of
- by way to
- instead for

詳解：in exchange for（以……作為交換）：供應商給 2% 折扣來換取提早付款。in return of 應為 in return for；by way to 應為 by way of；instead for 應為 instead of，這三個都是介係詞搭錯的常見錯誤。

### 2. Part 5（關係詞）

**The only supplier ______ could guarantee delivery within ten days was a small packaging firm in Porto.**

- ✅ that
- what
- whose
- whom

詳解：先行詞前有 the only 時，關係代名詞常用 that，而且空格後缺主詞（___ could guarantee），要主格：that。what 前面不能有先行詞；whose 後面必須接名詞；whom 是受格，不能當主詞。

### 3. Part 5（介係詞）

**The delay was caused by circumstances ______ the carrier's control, so no penalty will be charged.**

- ✅ beyond
- against
- past
- without

詳解：beyond one's control（無法控制的）是固定說法，所以運送商不必受罰。against 是「違反、反對」；past 表時間或位置的經過；without 是「沒有」，都和 control 搭不出「超出控制」的意思。

### 4. Part 5（關係詞）

**Customers may choose ______ delivery option best suits their schedule at no additional cost.**

- ✅ whichever
- whoever
- wherever
- however

詳解：空格後直接接名詞 delivery option，再接動詞 best suits，需要能當形容詞用的複合關係詞 whichever（無論哪一個）：whichever delivery option best suits。whoever 指人，不能修飾名詞；wherever 指地點；however 是「無論如何」，後面接形容詞或副詞。

### 5. Part 5（不定詞與動名詞）

**The extended warranty is well worth ______ for any café that runs its espresso machines more than ten hours a day.**

- ✅ purchasing
- to purchase
- purchase
- purchased

詳解：be worth + V-ing 表示「值得做某事」：is well worth purchasing。worth 後面不接不定詞 to purchase；purchase 若當名詞要加冠詞（worth the purchase），這裡沒有冠詞；purchased 是過去分詞，不能接在 worth 後面。

### 6. Part 6「新聞稿：物流公司與航空貨運合作」空格 [1]

```
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Contact: Helena Brandt, Communications Manager

SEATTLE (March 9) — Corvane Logistics announced today that it has entered into a three-year agreement with Northwind Air Cargo. Under the partnership, Corvane customers will gain access to overnight freight service between Seattle and twelve cities in Asia. ___[1]___, Northwind will dedicate two aircraft exclusively to Corvane shipments during the peak holiday season.

"Our clients have been asking for faster international options for some time," said Corvane CEO Marcus Webb. "This agreement allows us to meet that demand without ___[2]___ our own fleet of aircraft." ___[3]___

The new service will be available to customers beginning May 1. Corvane expects its international shipping volume to grow by 30 percent in the first year, and the company plans to hire forty additional warehouse employees to handle the ___[4]___ workload.
```

**空格 [1]**

- ✅ In addition
- Instead
- Even so
- Otherwise

詳解：前一句說客戶可以使用隔夜空運服務，這句再補充另一項合作內容（旺季專用兩架飛機），是「追加」關係，用 In addition（此外）。Instead（反而）表示取代前面的內容；Even so（即便如此）表示讓步；Otherwise（否則）表示條件相反，都和前後文的關係不合。

### 7. Part 6「新聞稿：物流公司與航空貨運合作」空格 [2]

```
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

SEATTLE (March 9) — Corvane Logistics announced today that it has entered into a three-year agreement with Northwind Air Cargo. Under the partnership, Corvane customers will gain access to overnight freight service between Seattle and twelve cities in Asia. ___[1]___, Northwind will dedicate two aircraft exclusively to Corvane shipments during the peak holiday season.

```

**空格 [2]**

- ✅ expanding
- expand
- expanded
- expansion

詳解：介係詞 without 後面要接動名詞，而且後面還有受詞 our own fleet，所以用 expanding（擴充）。expand 是原形動詞、expanded 是過去式或過去分詞，都不能直接放在介係詞後面；expansion 是名詞，後面不能再直接接受詞 our own fleet。

### 12. 聽力 Part 2

**You haven't seen my reading glasses, have you?**

- ✅ Did you check the conference room?
- Yes, I read it last night.
- A glass of water, please.

詳解：附加問句問有沒有看到自己的老花眼鏡，用反問「你找過會議室了嗎？」間接回答（暗示可能放在那裡）。「I read it last night」用 read 做同字陷阱；「A glass of water」的 glass 是「玻璃杯」，同字不同義。

### 13. 聽力 Part 2

**Our competitor just lowered their prices.**

- ✅ Do you know by how much?
- Lower the volume, please.
- The competition starts at noon.

詳解：說競爭對手剛降價，用反問「你知道降了多少嗎？」回應。「Lower the volume」用 lower 做同字陷阱（調低音量）；「competition」（比賽）和 competitor 同字根，答的是時間。

### 14. 聽力 Part 2

**Who designed the new company logo?**

- ✅ Wasn't it an outside agency?
- Yes, it looks much more modern.
- I'm taking a design course this fall.

詳解：Who 問誰設計了新標誌，用反問「不是外面的廣告公司做的嗎？」間接回答，這是高分常見的正解型態。「Yes, it looks…」疑問詞問句不能用 Yes 回答；「design course」用 design 做同字陷阱。

## 單字 20 個

1. **floor plan** (n.) 平面圖  
   The real estate agent showed us the floor plan of the second-floor office.  
   房仲向我們展示了二樓辦公室的平面圖。

2. **freelance** (adj.) 自由接案的、特約的  
   The magazine hires freelance photographers to cover overseas events and trade shows.  
   這本雜誌聘請特約攝影師報導海外活動和商展。

3. **diligent** (adj.) 勤奮的、認真的  
   Thanks to the diligent work of our auditors, the error was corrected quickly.  
   多虧稽核人員認真的工作，這個錯誤很快就被更正。

4. **escrow** (n.) 第三方託管（帳戶）  
   The buyer's deposit will be held in escrow until the sale is finalized.  
   買方的訂金將由第三方託管，直到交易完成。

5. **loyalty** (n.) 忠誠（度）  
   Our loyalty program rewards frequent shoppers with exclusive discounts and free shipping.  
   我們的會員忠誠計畫以專屬折扣和免運費回饋常客。

6. **variable** (adj.) 可變的、浮動的  
   The loan has a variable interest rate that is adjusted every six months.  
   這筆貸款採浮動利率，每六個月調整一次。

7. **patent** (n.) 專利  
   The engineering team has applied for a patent on its new cooling system.  
   工程團隊已為新的冷卻系統申請專利。

8. **slate** (v.) 預定、排定（be slated to / for）  
   Construction of the new terminal is slated to begin in early spring.  
   新航廈預定於初春動工。

9. **inclusive** (adj.) 包含在內的；包容的  
   The package price is inclusive of airfare, hotel accommodation, and daily breakfast.  
   套裝價格包含機票、飯店住宿及每日早餐。

10. **advisory** (adj.) 諮詢的、顧問的  
   The company formed an advisory committee to guide its expansion into Asian markets.  
   公司成立了諮詢委員會，為其進軍亞洲市場提供建議。

11. **defray** (v.) 支付、負擔（費用）  
   The company will help defray relocation costs for employees moving to the new office.  
   公司將協助負擔員工搬遷至新辦公室的費用。

12. **gratuity** (n.) 小費、服務費  
   A fifteen percent gratuity is automatically added for groups of eight or more.  
   八人以上的團體將自動加收百分之十五的服務費。

13. **noteworthy** (adj.) 值得注意的  
   One noteworthy feature of the new model is its extended battery life.  
   新機型值得注意的特色之一是電池續航力更長。

14. **undersigned** (n.) （文件末）簽署人、立約人  
   The undersigned agrees to the terms and conditions stated above.  
   立約人同意上述條款與條件。

15. **lead time** (n.) 前置時間、交貨期  
   The supplier needs a lead time of three weeks for custom orders.  
   供應商的客製訂單需要三週的前置時間。

16. **workforce** (n.) 勞動力；全體員工  
   Nearly half of the company's workforce is based in its overseas offices.  
   公司近半數員工派駐在海外辦公室。

17. **exchange rate** (n.) 匯率  
   Fluctuations in the exchange rate have increased the cost of imported parts.  
   匯率波動使進口零件的成本增加。

18. **inherent** (adj.) 固有的、內在的  
   There are risks inherent in expanding into unfamiliar foreign markets.  
   進軍陌生的海外市場本身就存在風險。

19. **fill in for** (phr.) 代替（某人）工作  
   Mr. Kim will fill in for the receptionist while she is on vacation.  
   接待員休假期間，將由 Kim 先生代班。

20. **legislation** (n.) 法規、立法  
   New legislation requires employers to provide paid sick leave to all workers.  
   新法規要求雇主為所有員工提供帶薪病假。
