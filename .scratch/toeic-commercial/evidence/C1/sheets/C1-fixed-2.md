## P7-17

文件 1：
```
Ferry Service to Link Port Ellis and Downtown

HARBOR CITY (April 28) - Commuters from Port Ellis will soon have an alternative to the crowded Coastal Highway. Bayline Ferries will launch a weekday service between the Port Ellis marina and Harbor City's Pier 9 on Monday, May 4.

Two new vessels, each able to carry 180 passengers, will depart every 30 minutes between 6:00 and 9:00 A.M. and return between 4:00 and 7:30 P.M. Morning ferries will make a brief stop at Grant Pier, near the Harbor City Convention Center, before continuing to Pier 9. The trip will take about 35 minutes.

A one-way ticket will cost $6.50, and a monthly pass will be available for $150. Passengers may bring bicycles on board for an additional $1 per trip.

"Every car we take off the highway helps," said Bayline chief executive Gloria Fuentes. "We expect the service to ease congestion noticeably within its first year."

Tickets go on sale online on Friday, May 1.
```

Thank you for your April 28 article about our new Port Ellis commuter ferry. I would like to clarify two points so that your readers can plan their trips.

In addition, I am pleased to announce that, to celebrate the launch, bicycles will travel free of charge throughout the month of May.

Nathan Osei
Director of Communications, Bayline Ferries
