# C1 單字檢查表

逐條檢查拼字、詞性、中文字義（多益語境）、例句用法與翻譯。有問題的寫 `編號: 問題與建議修正`；沒問題的不用列。

1. absenteeism (n.) 缺勤（率） — The HR department introduced flexible hours to reduce absenteeism among factory workers. / 人資部推出彈性工時，以降低工廠員工的缺勤率。
2. accomplished (adj.) 技藝純熟的、有成就的 — The keynote will be delivered by an accomplished architect with thirty years of experience. / 主題演講將由一位擁有三十年經驗、成就卓著的建築師主講。
3. accordingly (adv.) 相應地、因此 — Fuel costs have risen sharply, so we have adjusted our delivery fees accordingly. / 燃料成本大幅上漲，因此我們已相應調整運費。
4. accreditation (n.) （官方）認證、評鑑合格 — The laboratory received accreditation from the national quality standards agency last month. / 該實驗室上個月獲得國家品質標準機構的認證。
5. accumulate (v.) 累積、積聚 — Members accumulate reward points every time they book a room through our website. / 會員每次透過我們的網站訂房都能累積紅利點數。
6. acknowledge (v.) 確認（收到）；承認 — Please acknowledge receipt of this contract by signing and returning the attached form. / 請簽署並寄回附件表格，以確認已收到此合約。
7. adequate (adj.) 足夠的、適當的 — The warehouse does not have adequate storage space for the holiday inventory. / 倉庫沒有足夠的空間存放節慶期間的庫存。
8. administer (v.) 管理、執行；施行（測驗） — An outside firm will administer the employee pension plan starting in January. / 自一月起，員工退休金計畫將由外部公司負責管理。
9. advisory (adj.) 諮詢的、顧問的 — The company formed an advisory committee to guide its expansion into Asian markets. / 公司成立了諮詢委員會，為其進軍亞洲市場提供建議。
10. affix (v.) 貼上、附加 — Please affix the shipping label to the top of each package before pickup. / 收件前，請將貨運標籤貼在每個包裹的頂部。
11. aisle (n.) （商店、飛機的）走道 — Cleaning supplies have been moved to the aisle next to the pharmacy counter. / 清潔用品已移到藥局櫃檯旁的走道。
12. alliance (n.) 聯盟、結盟 — The two airlines formed an alliance to share routes and frequent flyer programs. / 這兩家航空公司結盟，共享航線與飛行常客計畫。
13. allowance (n.) 津貼、補助；限額 — Field staff receive a daily meal allowance when they travel for client visits. / 外勤人員出差拜訪客戶時可領取每日餐費津貼。
14. annex (n.) 附屬建築、配樓 — The training sessions will be held in the annex behind the main building. / 培訓課程將在主樓後方的附屬建築舉行。
15. apparel (n.) 服飾、服裝 — The retailer plans to expand its line of outdoor apparel this spring. / 這家零售商計畫在今年春季擴充戶外服飾產品線。
16. appliance (n.) （家用）電器 — All units in the new apartment complex come with energy-efficient kitchen appliances. / 新公寓社區的每戶都配有節能廚房電器。
17. applicable (adj.) 適用的、相關的 — Prices listed on the website do not include applicable sales taxes. / 網站上標示的價格不含適用的營業稅。
18. applicant (n.) 申請人、應徵者 — Each applicant must submit two letters of reference along with a current resume. / 每位應徵者都必須連同最新履歷提交兩封推薦信。
19. appoint (v.) 任命、指派 — The board appointed Ms. Okafor as chief financial officer effective next month. / 董事會任命 Okafor 女士為財務長，下個月生效。
20. approximately (adv.) 大約 — The renovation of the lobby will take approximately six weeks to complete. / 大廳整修約需六週才能完成。
21. archive (v.) 將…歸檔保存 — All signed contracts are scanned and archived in the company's secure database. / 所有簽署的合約都會掃描並歸檔於公司的安全資料庫中。
22. as of (phr.) 自…起、截至…為止 — As of July 1, all expense reports must be submitted through the online portal. / 自 7 月 1 日起，所有費用報告都必須透過線上平台提交。
23. asset (n.) 資產；有價值的人或物 — Her fluency in three languages is a valuable asset to our sales team. / 她能說流利的三種語言，是我們業務團隊的寶貴資產。
24. assortment (n.) 各式各樣、什錦 — The hotel breakfast buffet offers an assortment of pastries, fruit, and cereals. / 飯店的早餐自助餐提供各式各樣的糕點、水果和穀片。
25. assurance (n.) 保證；（品質）保證 — Our quality assurance team tests every batch before it leaves the factory. / 我們的品保團隊會在每批產品出廠前進行檢測。
26. attire (n.) 服裝、衣著 — Business attire is required for all staff attending the shareholders' meeting. / 所有出席股東大會的員工都須穿著商務服裝。
27. automate (v.) 使自動化 — The firm automated its invoicing system to reduce errors and save time. / 公司將開立發票的系統自動化，以減少錯誤並節省時間。
28. back order (n.) 缺貨待補（的訂單） — The printer cartridges you ordered are on back order until the end of the month. / 您訂購的印表機墨水匣目前缺貨，要到月底才會補貨。
29. balance sheet (n.) 資產負債表 — The accountant reviewed the balance sheet before the quarterly meeting with investors. / 會計師在與投資人的季度會議前審閱了資產負債表。
30. bankruptcy (n.) 破產 — After years of declining sales, the electronics chain filed for bankruptcy. / 在銷售連年下滑後，這家電子連鎖店申請破產。
31. banquet (n.) 宴會、正式餐會 — The annual awards banquet will take place in the hotel's grand ballroom. / 年度頒獎宴會將在飯店的大宴會廳舉行。

37. brainstorm (v.) 腦力激盪 — The marketing team met to brainstorm ideas for the holiday advertising campaign. / 行銷團隊開會腦力激盪，構思節慶廣告活動的點子。

40. bundle (v.) 搭配銷售、捆綁 — The software is bundled with a one-year technical support package at no extra cost. / 該軟體免費搭配一年期的技術支援方案。
41. bylaw (n.) （公司）章程、內部規章 — According to the company bylaws, the board must meet at least four times a year. / 根據公司章程，董事會每年至少必須開會四次。
42. capacity (n.) 容量、產能；身分 — The new plant will double our production capacity by the end of next year. / 新廠將在明年底前使我們的產能倍增。
43. carrier (n.) 貨運業者；電信業者 — We switched to a different carrier for international shipments to reduce delivery times. / 我們的國際貨運改用另一家貨運業者，以縮短運送時間。
44. cash flow (n.) 現金流量 — Offering early payment discounts has helped the company improve its cash flow. / 提供提早付款折扣，幫助公司改善了現金流量。
45. claim (n.) 索賠、理賠申請 — Customers must submit a claim within thirty days if their shipment arrives damaged. / 若貨物送達時損壞，顧客須在三十天內提出索賠。
46. clause (n.) （合約）條款 — The contract includes a clause that allows either party to terminate with sixty days' notice. / 合約中有一條款，允許任一方提前六十天通知後終止合約。
47. clerical (adj.) 文書的、辦事員的 — The temporary worker will handle clerical tasks such as filing and data entry. / 這位臨時員工將處理歸檔和資料輸入等文書工作。
48. commission (n.) 佣金；委託 — Sales representatives earn a five percent commission on every contract they close. / 業務代表每談成一份合約可獲得百分之五的佣金。

367. verbal (adj.) 口頭的 — A verbal agreement is not enough; we need the terms in writing. / 口頭協議並不夠，我們需要書面條款。
368. vicinity (n.) 附近、鄰近地區 — There are several affordable hotels in the vicinity of the convention center. / 會議中心附近有幾家價格實惠的飯店。
369. visibility (n.) 能見度；曝光度 — Sponsoring the event will increase our brand's visibility among young shoppers. / 贊助這場活動能提高我們品牌在年輕消費者間的曝光度。
370. void (adj.) 無效的 — The warranty becomes void if the device is repaired by an unauthorized shop. / 若裝置由未經授權的店家維修，保固即失效。
371. volatile (adj.) 波動大的、不穩定的 — Fuel prices have been highly volatile over the past few months. / 過去幾個月燃料價格波動很大。
372. walk-in (adj.) 免預約的、臨時上門的 — The clinic accepts walk-in patients on weekday mornings, but appointments are recommended. / 診所平日上午接受免預約病患，但建議事先預約。
373. wane (v.) 減弱、衰退 — Interest in the product began to wane after a cheaper competitor appeared. / 較便宜的競品出現後，市場對該產品的興趣開始減退。
374. windfall (n.) 意外之財、意外收益 — The sale of the unused land provided a windfall that funded the new research lab. / 出售閒置土地帶來一筆意外收益，為新研究實驗室提供了資金。
375. word of mouth (phr.) 口碑、口耳相傳 — Most of our new clients find us through word of mouth. / 我們大多數的新客戶都是透過口碑找上門的。
376. workforce (n.) 勞動力；全體員工 — Nearly half of the company's workforce is based in its overseas offices. / 公司近半數員工派駐在海外辦公室。
377. workstation (n.) 工作站、個人工作區 — Each workstation is equipped with two monitors and an adjustable chair. / 每個工作區都配備兩台螢幕和一張可調式座椅。
378. worthwhile (adj.) 值得的 — The training course was expensive, but most participants felt it was worthwhile. / 這門培訓課程雖然昂貴，但多數學員認為很值得。
379. write off (phr.) 註銷、勾銷（壞帳） — The firm had to write off several unpaid invoices from a bankrupt client. / 公司不得不將一家破產客戶的數張未付發票列為壞帳註銷。
380. year-end (adj.) 年終的、年底的 — All departments must submit their year-end reports by December 15. / 所有部門都必須在 12 月 15 日前提交年終報告。
