## L34-12（Part 3）

M: Hello. I run a small theatre company, and our rehearsal hall is being refurbished for three months. We need somewhere to keep our costumes and some scenery.
W: How much space are you thinking of?
M: The scenery panels are quite tall, but a unit of three metres by three metres should be enough. We'll be coming in most days to collect things for performances, though.
W: Then I'd recommend one on the ground floor. The lift to the first floor is fairly small, so getting large items up there can be a struggle.
M: That makes sense. Could I come and see it this afternoon?
